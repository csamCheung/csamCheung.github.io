Planner Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Planner (on the App Store as "Planner: Day & To-do") is a paper-style day planner for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: Planner doesn't collect any of your data.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you add to Planner is stored only on your device:

  • your timeline blocks and whether they're done
  • your spending entries and amounts
  • your month, week and day notes
  • your to-dos
  • your theme and paper settings

This information is not sent to us or anyone else. Planner makes no network connections. If you delete the app, this data is deleted too.

Feedback

If you tap Feedback in Settings, Planner opens WhatsApp so you can send a message. The app itself sends nothing. Anything you send in WhatsApp is covered by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No advertising
  • No selling or sharing of data

Children

Planner doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
