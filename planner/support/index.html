Common questions

How do I move between Month, Week and Day?

Use the Month / Week / Day switch at the top of the Plan tab, or tap a day in the month or week to open it. On Month, swipe left or right on the calendar to change month. Tap Today to jump back to today.

How do I add something to my day?

Open a day, choose Timeline and tap the round + button. Give it a name, a start time and a length. Planner shows how much free time is left between blocks.

How do I tick off or delete a block or to-do?

Tap the circle to mark it done. Swipe left on it to delete it. To remove a spending entry, tap the × next to it.

Where do I write notes?

Month and week notes are on by default. Tap "+ Notes" at the bottom of a page to add a notes page, or remove it from the notes card.

Why is my currency wrong?

Planner uses your iPhone's region for currency. Change it in iPhone Settings › General › Language & Region.

Can I change the colours?

Yes. Tap the gear for Settings and pick one of 12 themes and a paper style (plain, dotted, lined or squared).

Is my data backed up or synced?

Planner keeps everything on your iPhone and doesn't sync between devices. Your data is included in your normal iPhone backup.

Is my data private?

Yes. Nothing leaves your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
