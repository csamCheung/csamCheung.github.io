Common questions

How do I add my pet's photo?

Open Settings and tap the photo under Your buddy, or tap Add under Photos. You can add several; Timer-Pet uses a different one each session.

How do I see the countdown on my Lock Screen?

Start a timer and lock your phone. If you don't see it, go to iPhone Settings › Timer-Pet and make sure Live Activities is on.

How do I add the Home Screen widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, search for Timer-Pet and tap Add Widget.

I didn't hear a sound when the timer ended.

Check that your ring/silent switch isn't on silent, and that notifications are allowed in iPhone Settings › Timer-Pet › Notifications. You can choose the sound in Timer-Pet › Settings › Timer sound.

What's free and what's in the unlock?

Your first 3 sessions include everything. After that, the plain timer stays free forever. A one-time unlock keeps your pet's photos, goals and breaks. It's not a subscription.

I bought the unlock. How do I get it back on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you bought it with and open Timer-Pet; the unlock usually comes back on its own. If it doesn't, open the Unlock screen and tap Restore Purchase.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your photos, goals and settings stay on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
