Timer-Pet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Timer-Pet is a focus timer for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: Timer-Pet doesn't collect any of your data.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you add to Timer-Pet is stored only on your device:

  • photos of your pet that you choose
  • your pet's name
  • your goals and their dates
  • your words of encouragement
  • your session history and settings

This information is not sent to us or anyone else. The Lock Screen, Dynamic Island and Home Screen widget show it using storage on your own iPhone. If you delete the app, this data is deleted too.

Photos

You choose photos with Apple's photo picker. Timer-Pet only gets the photos you pick, not the rest of your library. They are cropped and saved inside the app on your device.

Notifications

Timer-Pet uses local notifications to tell you when a session or break ends. They are scheduled on your iPhone; no server is involved.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment. We never see your payment details, and we don't receive personal information about you from Apple.

Feedback

If you tap "Send me your thoughts" in Settings, Timer-Pet opens WhatsApp with a message ready for you to send. The app itself sends nothing. Anything you send in WhatsApp is covered by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No advertising
  • No selling or sharing of data

Children

Timer-Pet doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
